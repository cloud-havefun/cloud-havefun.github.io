Overview

Mang Den Discovery — a travel company based in Mang Den, in Vietnam's Central Highlands. As their videographer and editor, I filmed and cut a series of short films showcasing the region's untouched landscapes and warm local communities — footage used to promote tourism across the area.

RoleVideographer / Editor
ClientMang Den Discovery
Runtime6 videos
ToolsPremiere · DaVinci · After Effects
Format16:9 · 4K
Selected Videos
Video — 01 Kon Tum Reservoir — Part I 02:59
A remote hydropower lake tucked deep in the highlands, still wild enough to shelter rare species. Most of this film follows the crew traveling by boat across the water before making camp on a small island in the middle of the lake. 2023
Video — 02 Kon Tum Reservoir — Part II 04:04
The second chapter of the reservoir trip — quieter mornings on the water, the stillness of the surrounding primary forest, and everyday moments from island camp life. 2023
Video — 03 The Terraced Fields 03:11
Filmed on a trip through Ngoc Linh, Kon Tum, this piece follows local farmers at work on the terraced rice fields — sowing seedlings and transplanting rice by hand across land too steep and narrow for machinery. 2023
Video — 04 Son Tay 02:38
A trip through Son Tay, a mountainous stretch bordering Kon Tum, home mainly to the Cor ethnic community. The film moves through steep, layered ridgelines and quiet villages, spending time with the people who call this remote corner of the highlands home. 2024
Video — 05 Golden Harvest 02:22
Filmed during harvest season on the terraced fields of the Central Highlands, this piece follows local farming families cutting rice by hand — including their children, who join in after school to help carry, gather, and thresh alongside their parents. 2024
Video — 06 One Man's Way 01:46
A personal film made for a former boss — following him hiking, camping, and wandering through some of Mang Den's quieter, lesser-known corners. Less a promotional piece and more a keepsake: a record of solitude, slow mornings, and time spent somewhere he loves. 2024

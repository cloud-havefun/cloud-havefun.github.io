Overview

JTAI is an enterprise AI platform built for legal services organizations — document intelligence, grounded chat, and deployable agents for legal teams. I edited a series of product videos explaining and showcasing the platform's core features.

Process

Every video starts as a storyboard before it becomes a final cut. The client sends a screen-recorded walkthrough of the JTAI platform, and every box, icon, and UI element is redrawn from scratch to animate — mapping out pacing, key screens, and voiceover beats before touching the timeline.
